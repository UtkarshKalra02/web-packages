Terms

Clear terms, no surprises.

  • Payment is 50% in advance and 50% before the website goes live.
  • The client provides the logo, photographs and business details. Delivery timelines start when these are received.
  • Domain and hosting are included for the first year and renew at actual cost from the second year.
  • Prices are quoted before applicable taxes.
  • Paid advertising, professional photography and copywriting beyond the listed pages are quoted separately.
  • Search rankings depend on competition and on Google's own systems, so specific rankings or lead numbers are not guaranteed.
  • Backlinks are built through genuine listings and outreach. Paid link schemes are not used, as they breach Google's spam policies and can get a site penalised.
